Connect your treadmill

Close the manufacturer’s app first, the treadmill usually allows only one connection at a time.

If the belt is still running, the app offers to stop it first.
Connection lost. The belt may still be running!

The app can no longer stop it. Stop it on the treadmill: press the stop button or pull the safety key.

The treadmill did not take the stop. The belt may still be running!

Press Stop again, or stop it on the treadmill: press the stop button or pull the safety key.

Without a treadmill or without Web Bluetooth you can try the demo.

If the connection drops, this app cannot stop the belt. Then you can only stop it on the treadmill: stop button or safety key. Keep both within reach.

                      Privacy

                      No accounts, no server, no tracking, no external scripts or fonts. Only if you show the map of a route, it loads map data from a map server (by default OpenFreeMap, tiles.openfreemap.org), which sees your IP address and the part of the map you look at. Settings (limit, ramp, language, theme, accepted notice) are stored only locally in this browser. The saved runs stay on this device too.
